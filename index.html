Displacement & Damage Map Quick Guide

What the tool shows

This tool maps population, displacement, conflict proximity and damage to public institutions across South Lebanon and Nabatiyeh at cadastre, district or governorate level. Data come from the governorate population & IDP sheets (21 Sep 2026), the LRC Preliminary Assessment (Jun–Jul 2026) and a GeoAI satellite check.

Quick start

  1. Pick a resolutionUse the Administrative resolution buttons to set the map unit.
  2. Turn on an indicatorUnder Displacement & Population, pick one indicator. “Color” changes the ramp.
  3. Read the damageDots are public institutions coloured by damage level. Filter levels with the chips.
  4. Click an areaThe Analysis tab shows the figures for the clicked cadastre, district or governorate.
  5. See the GeoAI checkOpen GeoAI and fly to Khiam to compare AI-detected buildings with the field report.
