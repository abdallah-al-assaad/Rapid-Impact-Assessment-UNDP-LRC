About this map

The map brings together three information sources on the 2026 escalation in South Lebanon and Nabatiyeh: governorate population and displacement sheets, the LRC Preliminary Assessment of municipalities and public institutions, and a GeoAI analysis of satellite imagery. It is built for coordination and prioritisation by the UNDP Lebanon Information Management team.
